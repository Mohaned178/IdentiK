# Self-hosted-first, one artifact, Organization as first-class tenancy unit

The product is an Identity Provider for teams whose compliance, security, or data-ownership requirements force identity data into infrastructure they control — "Keycloak without the pain" — not an Auth0/Clerk clone. We ship one codebase in two operational modes: self-hosted single-tenant as the primary artifact, with a possible future hosted multi-tenant mode. That future is made compatible not by planning a rewrite but by making Organization a first-class, mandatory concept from day one — every domain concept carries its Organization scope, so the hosted mode is the same artifact operated by us at many-Organizations-per-Instance. Rejected: pure hosted SaaS (contradicts the data-control customer); pure single-tenant data model (forces a rewrite to ever host).
