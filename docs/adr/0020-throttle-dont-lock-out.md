# Uniform messaging, throttling, no hard lockout — visibility instead of self-DoS

Sign-up and sign-in responses are uniform in shape and timing whether or not an email exists — nothing is confirmed at the HTTP layer (the uniqueness refusal happens by email, not by response differential; the enumeration leak accepted in ADR-0005 is mitigated rather than amplified). Credential attacks are met with rate limiting and escalating delay, per-source and per-Identity. Hard lockout ("locked after 5 failures") deliberately does not exist: it is a denial-of-service primitive that lets any attacker log Mohamed out of existence, it buys little against distributed attackers, and it would create a second, cheaper suspension no Administrator invoked. Failed authentication attempts are captured as audit events carrying source and targeted Identity, so credential campaigns are visible in the dashboard while the platform declines to auto-punish: visibility instead of self-inflicted denial of service.
